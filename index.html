About me

I am a first-year PhD student at the University of York, supervised by Dr Jihong Zhu. My research focuses on deformable object manipulation (DOM) and contact-rich manipulation.

Previously, I completed my master's studies at King's College London, where my research focused on tactile and force sensing.

Research interests

  • Deformable object manipulation
  • Contact-rich robotic manipulation
  • Tactile and force sensing
