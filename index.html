King's College London, London, UK.

I am a KCL student majoring in tactile sensing. My interests lie in enabling robots to understand contact through visuo-tactile perception, force estimation, and contact-rich manipulation.

Prior to joining KCL, I earned my Bachelor's degree from Xihang University under the supervision of Prof. Zhufeng Lei. My research primarily focused on underwater noise signal processing, hydraulic technology, and servo control.
